# Launch Instructions (Exact Steps)

## Step 1: Post the X Article

Copy the entire content from `X_ARTICLE.md` and post it as a long-form post (or thread) on X.

**Recommended format:**
- Post as one long article (X supports long posts now)
- Or break into 6-8 tweets if you prefer a thread

## Step 2: Quote Post the Department of War Post

Go to this post:
https://x.com/DeptofWar/status/2052723504336736284 (or the exact one you saw)

Click **Quote Post** and paste the link to your X article at the top.

Example quote text:
"Frustrated by the same blurry UAP videos in 2026. So I built something about it.

Full story + working open-source system here:"

Then paste the link to your X article.

## Step 3: Reply to Your Own Quote Post with the GitHub Link

Reply to the quote post you just made with:

"GitHub repo (ready to run with Docker):
https://github.com/aether-uap/aether-uap

Star it. Run it. Help improve it."

## Done.

This creates a clean, professional launch that:
- Tells the human story
- Shows the problem + solution
- Makes it easy for people to try
- Links everything properly
